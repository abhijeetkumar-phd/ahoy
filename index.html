All Aboard

Welcome, and thank you
for being here.

Some stories begin with grand gestures. Some with a sudden surprise. Ours began more quietly in a classroom at National Law University Odisha in 2010, with shared lectures, borrowed notes, group projects, and an unexpected friendship. Over the years, that friendship carried us through different cities, continents, careers, and every season in between. It became the one constant in our dynamic lives, until one day we realised we had already found the person we wanted to keep choosing.

For us, marriage is not the start of something entirely new. It is a natural continuation of a relationship built on companionship, mutual respect, laughter, and an unwavering belief in each other. Our story cannot be told through a single milestone — it lives in countless conversations, memorable journeys, friendly debates, and the ordinary moments that became extraordinary simply because they were shared.

"As we celebrate this day with the people who have shaped our lives, we are grateful that you are here with us. Here's to continuing the journey together, as Boatmates, for this lifetime and beyond." — Honey & Abhijeet

Meet the Bride

Honey

Honey continues to thrive in the demanding world of corporate law, approaching every challenge with determination and independence. Away from work, she finds joy in art, music, and the impossible task of window shopping without being tempted. Those closest to her admire her resilience, warmth, and ability to stand her ground while standing by the people she loves. She has been Abhijeet's classmate and confidante for sixteen years, proving that the strongest partnerships are often built long before they are recognised.

Meet the Groom

Abhijeet

Abhijeet traded legal practice for the classroom, finding his calling as an academic after beginning his career as a lawyer. He is happiest when travelling with a camera in hand, always searching for the next landscape, street, or fleeting moment worth capturing. Never one to stay comfortable for too long, routine has never been his strongest suit. Family and friends know him as someone they can rely on. To Honey, he has always been a dependable friend first — that remains the foundation of everything else.
